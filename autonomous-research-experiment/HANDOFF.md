## TL;DR for the next agent

Two frozen 77 M Marian models (`opus-mt-vi-en`, `opus-mt-en-vi`), one 10 k test set, one shared
candidate pool, eleven selection rules. The question was: **is latent agreement a usable reference-free
quality signal, and does it beat string agreement?** Answer:

- **As a scorer, yes**: ρ +0.478 vs +0.394, Δ +0.083 [+0.075, +0.092] (paired bootstrap over sentences).
- **As a selector, no**: BLEU 22.31 vs 22.29; both lose 0.55 BLEU to plain beam-8 top-1 (22.85).
- **The pool contains the answer**: oracle over the same 8 candidates = 26.23 BLEU; the best
  reference-free rule (chrF-MBR) captures 6 % of that headroom.
- **Free quality the project never used**: beam-8 instead of greedy = +1.01 BLEU.

Full numbers and conditions: `REPORT.md`. Pre-registration: `HYPOTHESES.md`. Mistakes: `PROGRESS.md`.

## Known gaps / what I would do next (in order)

1. **Span-level version of the question.** Everything here is sentence-level. The project's own
   negative results (food probes, sentence-level decision models) say sentence granularity is the
   wrong instrument; a span-level latent agreement (source phrase ↔ hypothesis phrase) is the natural
   sequel and would also unlock fact insertion.
2. **COMET-Kiwi as a utility — attempted, blocked.** `Unbabel/wmt22-cometkiwi-da` is a gated repo
   (`GatedRepoError 401`, no HF credentials on this machine). It is the fair upper bound for a
   *learned* reference-free signal; accept the licence, then `python -m src.kiwi` works as written.
3. **Pool diversity — measured, negative.** Adding 8 sampled candidates per row raised distinct
   candidates from 7.96 to 14.94 and *lowered* the BLEU ceiling (26.54 → 26.40); MBR was indifferent,
   round-trip rules lost ~1.4 BLEU. Do not re-run this expecting a win; if you want a bigger ceiling,
   improve candidate quality (beam width, model, adaptation), not count.
3. **A reference-free utility that is not COMET.** The oracle uses COMET both as teacher and judge;
   a COMET-Kiwi (QE) predictor would break that circularity.
4. **The culinary domain.** 1,402 recipes + 1,544 atomic facts exist in `research-phase01/data-work/`
   and were never used inside the translation pipeline. Nothing tonight touches them.
5. **en→vi.** All generation here is vi→en. The reverse direction has the same scripts
   (`gen.py` is direction-agnostic if `config.A_ID`/`B_ID` are swapped).
