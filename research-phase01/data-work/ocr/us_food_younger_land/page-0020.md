## Cultural Significance of Salmon

Native cultures in the Northwest regard salmon as essential, celebrated through various cooking traditions such as barbecuing and smoking. The Lewis and Clark expedition noted the river's salmon abundance, and commercial fishing, initially by Russians, became prevalent. Sadly, overfishing and habitat loss have led to a decline in salmon populations.

## Salmon Cooking Traditions

Local variations of salmon preparation emerged, with residents of Yachats emphasizing unique techniques like sweet-brining before barbecuing. Traditional feasts by Indigenous peoples showcased salmon cooked in smokehouses, highlighting their culinary heritage.
