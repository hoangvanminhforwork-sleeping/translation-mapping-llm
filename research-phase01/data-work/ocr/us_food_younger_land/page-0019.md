Overview of the Far West Cuisine

The Far West region consists of diverse cuisines from the Rocky Mountains and the Pacific Northwest, including parts of states like Montana, Wyoming, Idaho, Colorado, Utah, Nevada, Northern California, Oregon, and Washington. This area is known for its abundant agricultural products and seafood.
