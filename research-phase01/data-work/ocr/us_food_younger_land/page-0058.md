8.Question

What inspired the creation of 'Depression Cake' and how
does it exemplify the resourcefulness of people during
hard times?

Answer:'Depression Cake' was birthed from necessity when a
woman, lacking regular baking ingredients, creatively
utilized stewed raisins and pantry staples to make a cake.
This recipe is symbolic of the ingenuity and resilience of
individuals during the Great Depression, showcasing how
limited resources could still yield something enjoyable,
which also became part of the communal culture surrounding
food.

Far West, providing a connection to the land and its history.
