How does the tradition of salmon preparation among
Native Americans differ from contemporary methods?
Answer:Indigenous traditions in the Northwest, such as
barbecuing salmon on cedar planks or smoking it, emphasize
sustainability and cultural reverence, differentiating it from
modern fishing practices which often prioritize profit and
may contribute to overfishing. The Native methods not only
serve practical needs but also symbolize fertility and
community identity.
