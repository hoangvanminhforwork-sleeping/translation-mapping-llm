1.Question
What historical significance do clams hold in New England culinary culture?
Answer:Clams are highly valued in New England, often more than oysters, dating back to the indigenous populations who consumed them regularly. European settlers also relied on clams for sustenance, and they have been integral to local traditions like the clambake. The cultural and economic importance of clams is emphasized by their inclusion in various regional dishes and celebrations.
