## Cultural Eating Practices in New Mexico

In New Mexico, "la merienda" represents the midday meal enjoyed outdoors, characterized by a range of flavorful dishes imbued with local traditions. This informal meal fosters familial bonds and serves as a comforting reminder of home-cooked foods.

## Choctaw Indian Culinary Practices

The Choctaw people's culinary practices highlight traditional dishes that use locally sourced ingredients and time-honored methods, often involving corn and various meats. Their communal feasts, like the “funeral cry,” mingle food with deeply significant cultural rituals.
