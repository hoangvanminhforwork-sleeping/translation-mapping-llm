## Wild Game and Foraged Foods

Game meats such as beaver tails and wild ducks feature in local diets, often prepared using traditional methods. Wild food foraging remains a cultural practice, with ingredients like dandelion greens and wild berries becoming a part of Western diets.

## Historical Dining Practices

Community events like annual salmon barbecues and pioneer dinners illustrate the social fabric and collective culinary practices of the Far West's inhabitants. Traditional dishes, particularly those raised from pioneers’ roots, such as salmon, potato salad, and desserts, remain staples at gatherings.
