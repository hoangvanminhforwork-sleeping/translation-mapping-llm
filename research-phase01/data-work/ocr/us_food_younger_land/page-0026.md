## Health Food Movement in Los Angeles

Los Angeles has positioned itself as a hub for health food, where numerous businesses cater to a clientele increasingly focused on dietary health. Many products are marketed based on nutritional content, showcasing a blend of old and new culinary practices, like "enriched" bread and capsules of vegetable powders that users consume for their health benefits.

## Mexican Influences on California Cuisine

Mexican cuisine significantly influences Southern California, leading to popular dishes like tacos and burritos becoming staples in American dining. These dishes, initially made using traditional methods, evolved as manufacturing processes introduced new tools, thus expanding their popularity beyond the Hispanic community.
