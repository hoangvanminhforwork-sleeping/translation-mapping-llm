1.Question
What distinguishes the cuisines of the Far West region, particularly in terms of ingredients and notable dishes?
Answer:The Far West region uniquely combines the eclectic Rocky Mountain cuisine and the rich offerings of the Pacific Northwest. Key ingredients include game meats like elk, wild duck, and even exotic options like beaver tail. The region’s abundant agricultural outputs also lead to signature dishes featuring salmon — treasured by indigenous cultures and early settlers alike. Curiously, salmon used to be affordable and plentiful, while today it is viewed as a luxury due to overfishing and environmental pressures.
