Eating in Vermont

- Vermonters prefer traditional meal times with breakfast, dinner at noon, and supper in the evening.
- Meals are hearty and focus on filling dishes rather than sophisticated or fancy foods.
- Signature foods include spiced pickles, pickled tomatoes, and hearty breakfast options like griddle cakes and sausage, often accompanied by Vermont's famous maple syrup.

Rhode Island May Breakfast

- Originating from the Oaklawn Baptist Church in the late 19th century, this breakfast tradition featured a variety of foods served in a communal setting, establishing social connections.
- The fare included cold ham, chicken, and various baked goods, evolving into a promise of community and togetherness.
