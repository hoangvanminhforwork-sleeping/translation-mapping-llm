THE NORTHEAST EATS

Overview

The Northeast of the United States has a rich culinary history shaped by its diverse states, including New Jersey, New York, and New England. While the distinct cultures of New York City and New England create competition in various domains, including food, the role of food in the region's identity is noteworthy.
