## Chapter 5 Summary : THE SOUTHWEST EATS

The Southwest Eats

Overview of the Southwest Region

The Southwest of the United States has undergone significant demographic and culinary changes. In the early 20th century, the region's cuisine primarily consisted of a unique Chicano version of Mexican food, influenced by local ingredients and traditions.

Culinary Traditions of Iowa in Los Angeles

Iowa's cultural identity continues to thrive in Los Angeles through annual picnics celebrating their roots, which include traditional dishes like fried chicken, hams, various salads, and an array of desserts. The picnics foster community and reminiscence, showcasing the strong ties Iowans maintain with their heritage despite living far from home.
